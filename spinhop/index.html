A few useful answers.

Is this the full SpinHop game?

No. This is a simplified web timing mini-demo with original geometric artwork. The native game adds moving spinner fields, characters, coins, combos, Fever and seeded challenges.

How does the full game make money?

SpinHop is free to download with advertising and optional one-time purchases. Remove Ads stops automatic between-run ads; optional rewarded videos remain. The Supporter Pack includes Remove Ads and the six launch characters. Prices vary by storefront.

Where is my demo best saved?

Only in this browser’s local storage when available. It is separate from the app’s score and is never uploaded by the demo. Sharing occurs only when you tap Share demo best.
