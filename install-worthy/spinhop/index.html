About SpinHop: One Tap, Big Leaps

SpinHop is a one-tap arcade game for iPhone and iPad with local progress, characters, and seeded challenges. It is free to play with advertising and optional one-time purchases. Remove Ads stops automatic between-run ads; optional rewarded videos remain available by choice. Purchases do not change jump physics.
