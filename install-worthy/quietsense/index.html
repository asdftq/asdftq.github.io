About QuietSense: Apartment dB Meter

QuietSense processes sound levels on device without saving raw audio or transcribing speech. Nearby-room values are modeled estimates, not measurements of another room. It is not a certified, legal, or safety sound meter. Unlimited checks require QuietSense Pro.
