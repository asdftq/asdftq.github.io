What to put in your log.

For equipment, describe a fan, compressor or other observable source rather than guessing which neighbor caused it.

  1. Write the local date, start/end time and time zone. A device timestamp follows the device clock; it is not independently certified.
  2. Describe the sound, how long it lasted and whether it repeated. Mark an unknown source as unknown.
  3. Record the phone position and estimated average/peak if available. Use the same position for comparisons.
  4. Keep your own notes private. This website has no complaint form and receives no recordings, addresses or report contents.

A few useful answers.

What are quiet hours in Austin?

There is no single quiet-hours rule reproduced here. Check Austin’s current official guidance for the source, property and situation, plus your lease. Construction rules do not automatically apply to every household sound.

Does a noise log prove a violation?

No. It describes your observations. A city may require a particular measurement method, location, duration or other documentation. Ask the relevant authority what it accepts.
