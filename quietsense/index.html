IN THE IPHONE APP

See what you’ll use.

Phone readings are reference/comparison estimates, not certified measurements or legal certification. Accuracy varies by device, microphone, placement and room. Rules vary by city, property and lease; check the current local ordinance and your lease. This is general information, not legal advice.

A few useful answers.

What can I try free?

One 60-second room check, with its real report saved on your device. Reading that report does not require a subscription. Pro adds unlimited checks and other monitoring controls.

Can it measure my neighbor’s room?

No. The microphone reads sound at your phone. Any nearby-room values in the app are modeled estimates, not measurements of what a neighbor hears.

Does it record conversations?

No. Sound level is processed on device; raw audio is not saved, uploaded or transcribed.

Can I export a report?

The current published version stores reports in the app. A pending update adds a free CSV of the latest completed check. The downloadable web worksheet is a clearly fictional example, not a real measurement or a claim of PDF export.

Can I use a phone reading as legal proof?

Do not treat it as certified evidence or a decision about a violation. Accuracy varies by hardware and placement; timestamps follow the device clock. Check the current city process and lease, and ask the relevant authority what documentation it accepts.
