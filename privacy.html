Website privacy.

This policy covers www.asdftq.com, the Install Worthy welcome hub, and the app information pages hosted here. Derrick Cho operates this website. Each app has its own privacy policy; website measurement does not add analytics to the apps themselves.

Optional website analytics

If you choose Allow analytics, we load Google Analytics 4 to measure page visits, sessions, and engagement. Google receives technical browser and device information, an approximate region derived from your IP address, page titles and paths, and a shortened referral URL. Analytics cookies help distinguish visits. Google Analytics 4 does not log or store individual IP addresses. We disable Google signals, advertising personalization, and enhanced measurement of forms, searches, outbound links, and embedded media.

We remove query strings and fragments from the page URL sent to Analytics. Simple campaign labels from utm_source, utm_medium, utm_campaign, utm_term, utm_content, and utm_id may be sent separately for attribution. We never send feedback text, ratings, form fields, license keys, email drafts, or data from installed apps. We do not add a user ID or measure activity inside the apps.

Your choice

Analytics is off until you allow it. No thanks leaves the Google tag unloaded. We save only your analytics preference in this browser's local storage, so you do not have to choose on every page. Use Analytics settings at the bottom of any page to change your choice. Withdrawing permission stops measurement and removes this site's accessible Google Analytics cookies. A withdrawal does not erase data already collected. Browser privacy tools and blockers may also prevent Analytics from loading.

Hosting and other services

GitHub Pages hosts this website and may process normal technical request data, including IP addresses, to deliver it. Following a store, Substack, or Lemon Squeezy link takes you to that provider's service. They apply their own policies. The NoBadge feedback form only prepares an email draft when you choose; its contents are excluded from website analytics.

Read Google's privacy policy, how Google uses information from partner sites, and GitHub's privacy statement.

Contact

For questions about this website, contact Derrick Cho through the website's GitHub repository. Please do not post private information in a public issue.
