Live listing description:
One tap can take you further.

Ride a spinning fidget spinner, wait for your moment, then tap to leap. Land on the next spinner and keep going. Miss, learn the timing, and jump straight into another run.

FIND YOUR FLOW
Start with an easy rhythm, then face faster, smaller spinners as your streak grows. Read the angle, speed, and distance to choose your next landing. Every successful jump is another reason to chase your personal best.

CHASE THE BONUS BEAT
Every field offers a gold route. Quick, accurate landings build your score; gold routes also count toward coin milestones. Land quick hops to build a combo up to x5. Fill six Fever charges to activate the next three hops for double points. The route stays readable while your timing gets sharper.

CHALLENGE A FRIEND
Share your run code and a friend can play the same seeded course. Compare scores, then run it again to find a cleaner line.

A SMALL CAST WITH BIG PERSONALITY
Start with Frog. Save coins earned through play to unlock Duck, Capybara, Raccoon, Orange Cat, and Blob. Earn up to 5 coins per run, or double that run’s coins by completing an optional rewarded video. Characters change your look; timing is what carries you. Existing players keep their previously available characters and saved coins.

PLAY AT YOUR PACE
Quick runs, one-thumb controls, and adjustable sound, haptics, and camera effects make it easy to fit in one more attempt. Your progress and character collection are saved on your device.

OPTIONAL EXTRAS
SpinHop is free to play and includes advertising and optional one-time in-app purchases. Rewarded ads are your choice. Remove Ads stops automatic ads between runs; optional rewarded ads remain available. The Supporter Pack includes Remove Ads and all six launch characters. Purchases do not improve jump physics.
