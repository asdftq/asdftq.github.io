# Paywall treatment decision

The free check already gives a saved report; the audit's premise that it leaves nothing to keep is stale. Add a free latest-report CSV to make the result portable. Keep the existing 60-second allowance, free report access and Pro unlimited checks. No price or subscription product changes.

Do not advertise all-night logging, background monitoring, notes, PDF output or certified timestamp integrity: they are not implemented. CSV contains device-clock UTC start/end, active duration, estimates, environment/activity and accuracy limits. An actual documentation product would need searchable session history, user notes and a clear source/position record; free NIOSH and logging competitors are real pressure. A CSV alone is insufficient paid differentiation.
