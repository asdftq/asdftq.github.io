TimerBar is a minimal, beautiful clock with a live progress bar. It is perfect for presentations, meetings, focus sessions, and any timed workflow.

Features:
- Set a timer by duration or end time
- Fullscreen clock with an elegant progress bar
- Quick start, pause, restart, and stop controls
- Optional remaining-time display
- Always-on display support
- TimerBar+ customization with premium themes, fonts, and colors

Stay focused and stay in control with TimerBar.
