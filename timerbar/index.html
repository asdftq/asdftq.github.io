A few useful answers.

Is the web timer free?

Yes. Start, pause and reset it without signing up. It runs in your browser; no timer settings are sent to us.

What does TimerBar+ include?

The iPhone/iPad app’s paid monthly or yearly subscription adds premium themes, fonts and colors. The core clock and timer are free. Current regional prices appear in the App Store.

Will the web timer keep my screen awake?

This web timer does not prevent your device from sleeping or play a background alarm. Keep the page visible for rehearsal. The native app supports an always-on display.

Can I use it for a speech contest?

Use it for rehearsal and check your organizer’s current rules. The closing cue is not an official Toastmasters green/yellow/red contest signal sequence.

Can I save timers in the app?

Yes. The current app supports named presets and duration editing. The web timer keeps only the current page’s timer in memory.
