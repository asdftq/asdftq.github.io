# Familiar-app preview artwork

Finder, Safari, Mail, Messages, Reminders and Calendar are a fixed selection of
common bundled Mac apps, not an inventory of the owner's apps. Transparent 256px
icons are exported unchanged from Apple's bundled app artwork using
`swift nobadge/tools/export-demo-icons.swift nobadge/assets/demo-apps` on macOS.
App names and artwork belong to Apple; no affiliation or endorsement is implied.

The landing-page Dock and app-switcher scenes are HTML/CSS illustrations, not
recordings or evidence of individual app compatibility. Counts (24, 3, 5) are
fictional. The page labels the previews and preserves the macOS-managed badge
limitation. No personal app list, messages, notifications or desktop are used.
