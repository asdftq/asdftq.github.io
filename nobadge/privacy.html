Privacy, by keeping things local.

This policy describes the current NoBadge app and this website. NoBadge is made by asdftq.

What the app processes

NoBadge reads your macOS Focus configuration and notification settings on your Mac. It uses installed app names and icons to let you choose exceptions. It changes supported badge preferences and keeps a local recovery journal so it can restore the values it changed. It does not read message contents, documents, or browsing history.

Permissions

Full Disk Access allows broad file access. NoBadge uses this permission for Focus and notification settings. You can revoke it in System Settings. Restore your previous badge settings from NoBadge before revoking access or removing the app.

No app account or analytics

The app has no app account, advertising, analytics, tracking SDK, or private app backend. License validation uses Lemon Squeezy’s License API. Focus names, your app list, and badge preferences are not sent to us.

License activation

Activation and periodic license checks send your purchase license key and a random activation label or activation identifier to api.lemonsqueezy.com over HTTPS. We do not send your computer name, Focus names, app list, or messages. License credentials and the last successful verification are saved in this Mac’s Keychain and are not synced by NoBadge. A verified license works offline for up to 30 days. An invalid or disabled license stops new suppression; restoration remains available.

Local files and diagnostics

Settings, the recovery journal, and bounded diagnostic state logs are stored in your local Application Support folder. Diagnostic exports happen only when you choose to save them. The app shows the exact diagnostic text first. If you email support, your email provider processes that message and we receive the information you choose to include.

Updates and ratings

NoBadge checks our signed update feed daily by default. You can disable automatic checks in About and check manually instead. Update requests go to www.asdftq.com, hosted by GitHub Pages, which receives normal technical request information such as IP address and app user agent. NoBadge disables Sparkle system-profile reporting. Downloads are verified using update signatures and Developer ID signing; installation requires your choice. After sustained successful use, NoBadge may show a small rating/review invitation in Settings. It stores only a first-use date, up to three qualifying day identifiers, and the date and version of the last invitation locally. The invitation opens this website only when you click it. The feedback page keeps your optional rating and comments in the current browser page and opens an email draft only when you choose. It does not submit or publish them automatically, and does not save them in browser storage. If you send that draft, we receive your email and its contents.

Purchases through Lemon Squeezy

Lemon Squeezy is the merchant of record for NoBadge purchases and handles checkout, payment, tax, receipts and file delivery. It may share customer name, email address and order information with us for fulfillment and support. We do not receive your full payment card details. See Lemon Squeezy’s privacy policy.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. Optional Google Analytics measures website visits only after you allow it. It does not receive app data, feedback text, or email drafts. You can decline or change your preference using Analytics settings at the bottom of each page. See our website privacy policy for details. We do not add advertising or external fonts. GitHub may process technical request data to operate the hosting service; see GitHub’s privacy statement. Videos and images are hosted with the site. Optional newsletter links open Substack, which has its own privacy policy.

Your choices

Deactivate this Mac in License if you want to free its activation, then use Restore & Quit before removing NoBadge. After restoration, removing the app and its local Application Support/NoBadge folder removes its saved preferences and logs. Do not delete an active recovery journal before restoration finishes.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, email asdftqtq+nobadge@gmail.com. We update this policy when the app’s data practices change.
