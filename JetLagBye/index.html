Arrive rested. Adapt faster.

A plan built around your actual trip

Jet Lag Bye creates a personalized itinerary from your departure, arrival, normal sleep schedule, and time-zone change. Each plan brings sleep timing, light exposure, caffeine guidance, and optional melatonin timing into one clear timeline.

Your first complete travel itinerary per device is free, with no payment card required. Choose either one-way or round-trip travel.

Key Features

  • Personalized Travel Plans: Guidance calculated for your route, dates, sleep schedule, and direction of travel
  • One-Way and Round-Trip Support: A complete plan for the full itinerary you enter
  • Light and Sleep Timing: Practical windows for seeking light, avoiding light, sleeping, and waking
  • Caffeine and Optional Melatonin Guidance: Timing suggestions organized alongside the rest of your plan
  • Plan Progress and History: Check off actions and revisit saved plans on your device
  • Reset Sleep: Annual Unlimited members can build a recovery schedule even when they are not planning a flight
  • Private by Design: No account, developer server, analytics SDK, or advertising SDK; your trip and sleep information stays on your device
