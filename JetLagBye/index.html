A few useful answers.

Is my first plan free?

Your first complete one-way or round-trip itinerary is free as a one-time local offer, without a card. Additional plans use a Single Trip Plan purchase or Annual Unlimited. Reinstalling does not reset the offer.

What does the app plan?

Sleep, waking, light exposure and caffeine timing around your itinerary and usual sleep schedule. Recommendations use the correct departure or destination clock.

Can I save the plan for travel?

Yes. Revisit locally saved plans, check off actions and save the full itinerary as an image in Photos.

Do the route guides tell me exactly when to seek light?

No. Precise timing depends on your sleep schedule and flights. Large time changes and date-line crossings need special care; a route’s compass direction alone is insufficient.

Is it medical advice?

No. This is general educational information and individual results vary. Ask a qualified clinician before changing sleep routines or using sleep aids, including melatonin.
