A few useful answers.

What is the ORD to HND time difference?

On 2026-10-26, Tokyo Haneda is 14 hours ahead of Chicago. Seasonal offset examples: January 15, 2027: +15 hours; July 15, 2027: +14 hours (destination minus origin). The difference changes seasonally, so a saved offset can be wrong for another departure date.

Is this an westward trip?

The shorter geographic direction from ORD to HND is westward. Direction and civil-clock offset are different concepts, especially around the date line. Neither alone determines your personal light schedule.

Is the sample a personal plan?

No. It is an educational outline, not advice for your sleep schedule or health. The app uses your itinerary and usual sleep to create a plan; your first complete itinerary is free.
