Two or three days before departure

Look at the NRT evening row above beside the BOS clock. If feasible, make a modest sleep-schedule shift toward your destination without cutting your sleep short.

Flight day

Keep the ticket’s NRT departure date and BOS arrival date visible. Plan a realistic opportunity to rest; avoid relying on alcohol for sleep. Caffeine close to intended sleep can make rest harder.

First local day

Use Boston local time for meals, activities and the coming bedtime. This large civil-clock gap crosses a calendar boundary in many schedules. Do not turn the printed hour difference into hours of forced wakefulness. Check both arrival date and clock time before planning rest.

Following days

Recheck how rested you feel before adding demanding activities. General advice is not a promise of a fixed adjustment time; use your personalized schedule and clinical guidance when appropriate.

A few useful answers.

What is the NRT to BOS time difference?

On 2026-10-26, Boston is 13 hours behind Tokyo Narita. Seasonal offset examples: January 15, 2027: -14 hours; July 15, 2027: -13 hours (destination minus origin). The difference changes seasonally, so a saved offset can be wrong for another departure date.

Is this an eastward trip?

The shorter geographic direction from NRT to BOS is eastward. Direction and civil-clock offset are different concepts, especially around the date line. Neither alone determines your personal light schedule.

Is the sample a personal plan?

No. It is an educational outline, not advice for your sleep schedule or health. The app uses your itinerary and usual sleep to create a plan; your first complete itinerary is free.
