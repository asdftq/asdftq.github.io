Two or three days before departure

Look at the LHR evening row above beside the EWR clock. If feasible, make a modest sleep-schedule shift toward your destination without cutting your sleep short.

Flight day

Keep the ticket’s LHR departure date and EWR arrival date visible. Plan a realistic opportunity to rest; avoid relying on alcohol for sleep. Caffeine close to intended sleep can make rest harder.

First local day

Use Newark local time for meals, activities and the coming bedtime. This smaller long-haul clock difference can still disturb sleep. Anchor your first local evening to the destination date rather than treating tiredness as a cue to take a long late nap.

Following days

Recheck how rested you feel before adding demanding activities. General advice is not a promise of a fixed adjustment time; use your personalized schedule and clinical guidance when appropriate.

A few useful answers.

What is the LHR to EWR time difference?

On 2026-10-26, Newark is 4 hours behind London. Seasonal offset examples: January 15, 2027: -5 hours; July 15, 2027: -5 hours (destination minus origin). These two seasonal examples have the same difference; always check your own travel date.

Is this an westward trip?

The shorter geographic direction from LHR to EWR is westward. Direction and civil-clock offset are different concepts, especially around the date line. Neither alone determines your personal light schedule.

Is the sample a personal plan?

No. It is an educational outline, not advice for your sleep schedule or health. The app uses your itinerary and usual sleep to create a plan; your first complete itinerary is free.
