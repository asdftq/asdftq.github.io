Flight day

Keep the ticket’s HND departure date and ORD arrival date visible. Plan a realistic opportunity to rest; avoid relying on alcohol for sleep. Caffeine close to intended sleep can make rest harder.

First local day

Use Chicago local time for meals, activities and the coming bedtime. This large civil-clock gap crosses a calendar boundary in many schedules. Do not turn the printed hour difference into hours of forced wakefulness. Check both arrival date and clock time before planning rest.

A few useful answers.

What is the HND to ORD time difference?

On 2026-10-26, Chicago is 14 hours behind Tokyo Haneda. Seasonal offset examples: January 15, 2027: -15 hours; July 15, 2027: -14 hours (destination minus origin). The difference changes seasonally, so a saved offset can be wrong for another departure date.

Is this an eastward trip?

The shorter geographic direction from HND to ORD is eastward. Direction and civil-clock offset are different concepts, especially around the date line. Neither alone determines your personal light schedule.

Is the sample a personal plan?

No. It is an educational outline, not advice for your sleep schedule or health. The app uses your itinerary and usual sleep to create a plan; your first complete itinerary is free.
