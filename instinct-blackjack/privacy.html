Privacy Policy

Instinct Blackjack · Effective October 7, 2026

Practice on your device

The app stores preferences, first-choice accuracy, session history, coaching unlocks and a daily round count locally. These practice records stay on your device and are not sent to an app backend. There is no app account, chat, contacts access, microphone access or precise location permission.

Advertising and consent

Free mobile play uses Google AdMob interstitial and optional rewarded ads. Google's SDK may process IP addresses (which can infer approximate location), device or advertising identifiers, advertising interactions and diagnostic information to deliver ads, limit frequency, prevent fraud and measure performance. We request non-personalized ads. That setting does not mean the SDK collects no data. Where applicable, Google's consent form and privacy choices are available in Options. Subscribers do not receive ads. We do not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission or access IDFA without authorization.

See Google's privacy policy and Google's partner data practices.

Purchases

Apple or Google handles payment and billing details; we do not receive your payment card number. On iOS, Apple's StoreKit checks current subscriptions on your device. On Android, Google Play Billing provides current purchase ownership and the app acknowledges purchases directly with Google Play. We do not run a purchase verification server or receive your purchase tokens on an app backend.

Choices and deletion

You can reset training history in Options → Advanced → Data management. This retains preferences and today's free-round allowance. Removing the app removes its local data, subject to your operating system's backup settings. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your App Store or Google Play account. For data or privacy requests, contact asdftqtq+blackjack@gmail.com.

Audience and changes

The app is intended for adults practicing blackjack strategy and is not directed to children. We update this page when data practices change.
