Free daily games

You can start 20 free rounds per local calendar day. Split hands and corrected choices are part of the same round. The final round can finish before the next deal is blocked. Games refresh the next day. Ads and rewarded coaching do not add games.

Subscription and restoration

Instinct Coach unlocks unlimited games, removes ads and includes every coaching explanation. Use Restore Purchases with the same store account if access is missing. Check your internet connection for purchase verification. Manage billing and cancellation through Apple or Google Play.

Strategy and history

The default rules are six decks, dealer stands on soft 17, blackjack pays 3:2, US peek and double after split. Late surrender is off by default. Accuracy counts the first independent choice; corrections do not erase a missed choice. Session records save locally after 30 decisions and the full round finishes.
